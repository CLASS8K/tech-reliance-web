Privacy notice

How Tech-Reliance handles personal data sent through this website, under Malawi's Data Protection Act 2024.

Who we are

Tech-Reliance is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. Contact: our email address.

What we collect

  • Details you enter in the booking form: name, organisation, work email, phone number, sector, approximate number of data subjects, the service you are interested in, and any message.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider for security, such as IP address and request times.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.

Why we use it

To respond to your request, arrange your call and provide services you ask for. Our basis is your consent, given in the form, and our legitimate interest in replying to enquiries.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for up to 24 months, unless you become a client, in which case your engagement records are kept for as long as our contract and the law require.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. Our website host, database provider and email provider process it on our behalf. Some of these providers store data outside Malawi; where they do, we rely on safeguards that meet sections 38 to 40 of the Act.

How we protect it

Form submissions are stored in a database that website visitors cannot read. Access is limited to Tech-Reliance and protected by multi-factor authentication.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by contacting us. You may also complain to MACRA, the Data Protection Authority.
