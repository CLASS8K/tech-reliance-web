Built for the strictest deadline, so one process works everywhere

Breach deadlines range from "immediately" in Uganda and 24 hours in Zambia to 48 hours in Rwanda and 72 hours in most other countries. South Africa requires every security compromise to be reported, however minor. The kit's breach plan runs to a 24-hour clock, so your team learns one process, not twelve.

Common questions

Is this legal advice?

No. These are templates written by an information security and data protection practitioner. Adapt them to your organisation and have them reviewed against current law in each country before adoption. Tech-Reliance is not a law firm.

Which countries' annexes are available?

Annexes for South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya and Egypt come first, followed by Ghana, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, Zambia, Malawi, Botswana and Mauritius. Pan-African and consultant licence buyers receive every new annex at no extra cost.

What format are the files?

Editable Microsoft Word (.docx) files in a single ZIP download. They also open in Google Docs and LibreOffice.

Can I use it for my clients?

Only with the Consultant licence, which allows you to adapt the templates for your clients and remove our branding. You may not resell the templates as a template product.

What happens when a law changes?

Consultant licence holders receive updated documents by email while their licence is active. Single-country and Pan-African buyers receive updates for 12 months from purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days if you have not downloaded the files, or if a file is defective and we cannot fix it within 7 days. See our terms of sale.
