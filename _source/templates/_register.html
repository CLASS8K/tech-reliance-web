Register with MACRA s.41–42 Required if you process the data of more than 10,000 people in Malawi, or data of significance to the economy, society or security. Registration test and a complete filing pack.
Report breaches fast s.36–37 Notify MACRA within 72 hours, tell affected people when the risk is high, and keep a breach record. Breach response plan, notification templates and a rehearsal.
Secure the data s.35 Technical and organisational measures such as encryption, access control, regular risk assessment and testing. Security review mapped to ISO 27001, and monitoring if you need it.
Control transfers abroad s.38–40 Personal data may leave Malawi only where it stays adequately protected. Cloud and SaaS vendors count. Transfer register and vendor clause review.
Appoint a data protection officer Act and MACRA Handbook Organisations processing personal data at large scale need a DPO. DPO as a service, or training for your own appointee.
