DPO as a service: an outsourced data protection officer on a monthly retainer

Compliance is not a one-off project. Systems change, vendors change and staff change. We act as your data protection officer so your compliance keeps up, without a full-time hire.

Book a free exposure check

What you get

  • A named DPO for MACRA, your staff and the public.
  • Data subject requests handled: access, correction and deletion requests, answered on time and on record.
  • New systems and vendors assessed before they go live, with impact assessments where needed.
  • Breach support: on call to lead your response and draft the MACRA notification within the 72 hours.
  • Policy upkeep as the law, MACRA guidance or your operations change.
  • A quarterly compliance report for your board or audit committee.
  • Annual staff refresher training.

How it works

  1. BaselineWe start from a readiness sprint, or review your existing documentation if you already have it.
  2. AppointmentWe formalise the DPO role, contact details and reporting line.
  3. Monthly rhythmA monthly check-in, with requests and changes handled as they come in.
  4. Quarterly reportStatus, incidents, requests handled and the next quarter's priorities.

A good fit if

  • You process personal data at scale and need a DPO, but not a full-time one.
  • Your IT or compliance team is stretched and data protection keeps slipping.
  • You want someone independent of your IT function in the role.

Common questions

How is the retainer priced?

A fixed monthly fee based on your size, number of systems and expected volume of requests, agreed up front.

Do we need a readiness sprint first?

If you have no compliance documentation yet, yes. Otherwise we can start with a short review of what you have.

What if a breach happens out of hours?

You get a direct line for incidents. The 72-hour clock does not stop at weekends, and neither does our breach support.
