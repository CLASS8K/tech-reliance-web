Readiness sprint: become compliant with the Data Protection Act in 4 to 6 weeks

We map the personal data you hold, close the gaps against the Act, prepare your MACRA registration and leave you with the documents to prove compliance to your board, your donors and the regulator.

Book a free exposure check

What you get

  • Registration determination and MACRA filing pack, ready to submit.
  • Record of processing: what personal data you hold, why, where it lives and who can reach it.
  • Gap assessment with a red, amber or green rating for each obligation, and a prioritised fix list.
  • Policy pack tailored to you: data protection policy, retention schedule, data subject rights procedure, acceptable use and vendor policy.
  • Breach response plan with MACRA notification templates and a breach log.
  • Security measures review mapped to ISO 27001 controls.
  • Transfer register covering every cloud and SaaS vendor that holds data outside Malawi.
  • A completed data protection impact assessment for your highest-risk system, plus the template for future ones.
  • Staff awareness session (45 minutes) and a board readout.

How it works

  1. Week 1: kickoff and data mappingAn intake questionnaire, then a two-hour workshop with your IT, HR, finance and operations leads.
  2. Week 2: record of processing and registrationWe document what you hold and confirm whether, and how, you must register.
  3. Week 3: gaps, policies and breach planThe gap report, tailored policies, the breach plan and the security review.
  4. Week 4: training and readoutStaff session, board presentation and support with your MACRA filing.

Larger organisations with several systems or sites take up to 6 weeks.

A good fit if

  • You hold personal data on more than 10,000 people, or you are close to that number.
  • You have not yet registered with MACRA, or you registered without doing the groundwork.
  • An audit, a donor requirement or a board question needs a credible answer.

Common questions

How is the price set?

After the free exposure check we quote one fixed price based on the number of systems, sites and data types. The quote covers every deliverable listed here.

How much of our staff's time does it take?

About a day in total for your key people: the workshop, a few follow-up questions and the readout.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is compliance advisory. Where a legal opinion is needed, we will tell you and can refer you to a Malawian data protection lawyer.

What happens after the sprint?

You can run compliance yourself with the documents we leave, or keep us on as your outsourced DPO.
