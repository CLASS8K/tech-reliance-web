Process automation: take the paper and retyping out of one workflow at a time

Invoices, customs documents, applications and approvals still get printed, scanned and retyped. We automate those workflows with document reading, rules and notifications, with privacy designed in from the start.

Talk about a workflow

What you get

  • One automated workflow, from incoming document or form to the finished record, approval or report.
  • Document reading that pulls the fields you need from invoices, IDs, forms and shipping documents.
  • Notifications by email or WhatsApp when something needs a decision.
  • Privacy by design: only the data you need, stored where it should be, with access logged.
  • Handover documentation and training for the people who run it.

How it works

  1. Pick the workflowWe find the one that costs you the most hours or errors.
  2. Map itInputs, decisions, outputs and the data involved.
  3. Build and testOn your real documents, with your team checking the results.
  4. Run and improveLive use, with an optional retainer to monitor it and add improvements.

A good fit if

  • Staff spend hours retyping information from documents into systems.
  • Approvals stall because no one knows what is waiting for them.
  • You want automation without creating a new data protection risk.

Common questions

How is it priced?

A fixed price per workflow, based on its complexity, plus an optional monthly fee to run and improve it.

Do we need new software?

Usually not. We connect to the tools you already use where possible.
