Managed security: know about a breach in time to report it

The Act gives you 72 hours from the moment you become aware of a breach. Most organisations find out weeks later, or never. We give you round-the-clock detection and response, with a monthly review your board can understand.

Book a free exposure check

What you get

  • 24/7 threat detection and response across laptops, servers and cloud accounts, run with a specialist security operations centre.
  • Onboarding and tuning so alerts reflect real risk to your organisation, not noise.
  • Incident coordination: when something happens, we lead the response and connect it to your breach plan.
  • A monthly security report and review meeting.
  • A virtual CISO: security advice for management and the board, without a full-time hire.

How it works

  1. Security baselineWe assess your current setup and agree what to monitor.
  2. DeployLightweight agents on your devices and connections to your cloud services. No hardware to buy.
  3. TuneTwo to four weeks of adjusting detection to your environment.
  4. Run and reviewContinuous monitoring, with a monthly report and review meeting.

A good fit if

  • You hold sensitive data and have no one watching your systems outside office hours.
  • Building your own security operations team would cost too much.
  • Your board, regulator or donors want evidence that you can detect attacks.

Common questions

How is it priced?

Per user or device per month, plus a fixed monthly fee for the virtual CISO review. You get the full price after the security baseline.

Where is our data stored?

We agree data residency with you before deployment and record it in your transfer register, as the Act requires.

Do we need to replace our antivirus?

Usually the monitoring agent replaces it. We confirm this during the baseline.
