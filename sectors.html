How the Data Protection Act affects your sector

The obligations are the same for everyone. The data, the risks and the right starting point are not.

Microfinance and SACCOs

Data you hold
Customer identities, loan histories, guarantor details, mobile money transaction records.
Where the risk is
Thin IT teams holding large customer bases, with many staff able to reach records and data often shared with mobile money partners.
Where to start
Readiness sprint, then managed security for branch devices.

Insurers and pension funds

Data you hold
Policyholder and member records, beneficiaries, medical information for life and health cover.
Where the risk is
Health information is especially sensitive, and claims data moves between brokers, assessors and reinsurers, often abroad.
Where to start
Readiness sprint with a focus on transfers and processor contracts.

Hospitals, clinics and labs

Data you hold
Patient records, test results, prescriptions, payment and insurance details.
Where the risk is
Exposed health data causes the most harm, and clinical systems are often old and shared across many staff.
Where to start
Readiness sprint, Microsoft 365 security, then DPO as a service.

Universities and schools

Data you hold
Student and parent records, staff files, exam results and fee payments, including data on minors.
Where the risk is
Records on children need extra care, and admissions and fee systems are frequent targets.
Where to start
Readiness sprint and staff awareness training.

NGOs and donor programmes

Data you hold
Beneficiary registers, household surveys, biometric or ID data, health and protection case files.
Where the risk is
Vulnerable people's data, field collection on personal phones, and donor systems hosted abroad.
Where to start
Readiness sprint aligned to your donors' data requirements.

Logistics, clearing and trade

Data you hold
Client and consignee details, customs declarations, IDs and payment records.
Where the risk is
Documents passing through many hands and email inboxes, and data shared across borders.
Where to start
Readiness sprint combined with process automation for document handling.
